### ⚡ Key Features at a Glance

#### 1. 100% Cloud-Powered Scraping (Zero Server Bloat)
Unlike legacy plugins that execute resource-heavy web scrapers on your WordPress server, MarketPulse offloads all DOM parsing, JavaScript rendering (Playwright/Puppeteer), anti-bot mitigation, and algorithmic math to the high-performance **MarketPulse Cloud Infrastructure**. Your store stays lightning-fast and your hosting resources remain 100% dedicated to serving customers.

#### 2. 6 Intelligent Repricing Strategies
Tailor your pricing logic per product with surgical precision:
* **Beat Lowest Competitor:** Price lower than your cheapest competitor by a fixed amount (e.g., \$1.00 or 5%).
* **Match Lowest Competitor:** Price-match the lowest competitor penny-for-penny to maximize conversion.
* **Target Market Average:** Balance profitability and competitiveness by pegging your price to the average competitor price.
* **Match Highest Competitor:** Premium brand positioning — position your product as a luxury alternative.
* **Fixed Margin Above Cost:** Maintain healthy bottom-line profits with automated cost-plus markup.
* **Position-Based Rank:** Target the 2nd lowest or 3rd lowest price to stay competitive without triggering price wars.

#### 3. Ironclad Profit Floor & Ceiling Safeguards
Never sell at a loss again. Set a mandatory **Minimum Allowed Price (Floor)** and an optional **Maximum Allowed Price (Ceiling)**. No matter how low competitors slash their prices, MarketPulse will **NEVER** lower your price below your floor.

#### 4. Out-of-Stock Opportunity Pricing
When your competitors run out of inventory, why keep your price low? MarketPulse detects competitor stock-outs and automatically increases your price to your configured ceiling or a custom markup percentage — capturing maximum profit when market supply is constrained.

#### 5. Instant Mobile Alerts via WhatsApp & Telegram
Get real-time push notifications delivered straight to your smartphone the exact second a competitor changes their price or an automated repricing rule executes. Stay ahead of market shifts wherever you are.

#### 6. Live Competitor Price Inspector (⚡ Check Price)
Test and verify competitor links directly inside the WooCommerce product edit metabox. Click **"⚡ Check Price"** to trigger a real-time cloud scrape that fetches the competitor's current price, currency, and availability in under 3 seconds.

#### 7. Automated Cache-Busting
When MarketPulse updates a product price, it instantly purges the specific product page cache across **LiteSpeed Cache, WP Rocket, W3 Total Cache, WP Super Cache, Breeze, and Cloudflare**. Shoppers always see your accurate, competitive price immediately.

#### 8. Live Catalog Badges & Filterable Product Insights
See at-a-glance competitive indicators on your WooCommerce **Products > All Products** catalog:
* 🟢 **Winning:** Your store has the lowest price in the market.
* 🟡 **Price Dropped:** A competitor dropped their price and your price adjusted automatically.
* 🔵 **Opportunity:** Competitor is out of stock; profit opportunity captured.
* ⚪ **Monitoring:** Active tracking running smoothly.

#### 9. Built-in User Guide & Interactive FAQ in wp-admin
No need to leave WordPress to read documentation. A complete, beautifully formatted User Guide and searchable FAQ are integrated directly inside your admin screen (`WooCommerce > MarketPulse > User Guide`).

#### 10. Storefront 50/50 A/B Split-Testing Engine (Zero-Flicker CRO)
Stop guessing which price point or headline converts best. MarketPulse brings enterprise price elasticity and Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) straight to WooCommerce:
* **Zero-Flicker Server Rendering:** Price variations, titles, and CTA buttons are rendered natively on the server before the browser paints — no jarring layout shifts (CLS: 0.00) or client-side DOM replacement flickers.
* **50/50 Sticky Visitor Sessions:** Automatically allocates shoppers 50/50 between Control (Variant A) and Test (Variant B) using lightweight, GDPR-compliant cookie hashing.
* **4 Test Dimensions:** Test **Price Elasticity** (higher or lower test prices), **Product Titles**, **CTA Button Text** ("Buy Now" vs "Claim Deal"), or **Featured Hero Images**.
* **Real-Time Financial Lift Analytics:** Measure the exact financial impact with **Revenue Per Visitor (RPV)**, **Total Revenue Lift ($)**, **Add-to-Cart Lift**, and **Purchase Conversion Rates**.
* **1-Click Winner Crowning:** Once statistical confidence is reached, permanently apply the winning variation to WooCommerce with a single click.

### Automatic Installation (Recommended)
1. Log in to your WordPress admin dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Plugins > Add New**.
3. Search for **"MarketPulse"** or **"WooCommerce Repricing"**.
4. Click **Install Now**, then click **Activate**.
5. Go to **WooCommerce > MarketPulse** and click **"Connect to MarketPulse (1-Click)"**.

### Manual Installation
1. Download the `marketpulse-for-woocommerce.zip` file.
2. In your WordPress admin, go to **Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin**.
3. Choose the downloaded ZIP file and click **Install Now**.
4. Activate the plugin and follow the 1-Click pairing flow.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does this plugin slow down my WordPress site or freeze my server? =
Absolutely not! MarketPulse is built with an external companion architecture. All web scraping, headless Chrome browser execution, and anti-bot handling happen on MarketPulse's external cloud infrastructure (`usemarketpulse.com`). Your WordPress server only receives lightweight REST webhook updates with the new price, keeping your website blazing fast.

= Which competitor websites and marketplaces can I track? =
MarketPulse supports **Amazon, Daraz, eBay, Walmart, AliExpress, Target, Best Buy, and virtually any independent eCommerce storefront globally** (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, or custom websites).

= What happens if I manually change a product price in WooCommerce? =
Your manual price is fully respected! If you change a price manually (e.g. from \$80 to \$75), MarketPulse logs the change. If you have an active automated repricing rule, MarketPulse will re-evaluate competitor prices on the next scheduled cycle and calculate the optimal price based on your current rule parameters. If you ever want to lock a price permanently, simply uncheck the "Active" repricing toggle on that product.

= What happens if my server experiences downtime or a network glitch? =
MarketPulse is engineered with high resilience. Your WooCommerce store maintains full autonomy: customer checkouts, admin price edits, and standard operations are never blocked. If our cloud cannot reach your site due to host downtime, all price updates are safely queued in our Redis cloud queue and retried with exponential backoff once your site is back online.

= Does it support WooCommerce Variable Products? =
Yes! You can configure competitor URLs, floor prices, and repricing rules individually for each variation (e.g. different sizes, colors, or bundle packs).

= How does the 50/50 A/B Split-Testing feature work in WooCommerce? =
MarketPulse's A/B Split-Testing allows you to test different price points, titles, or CTA buttons to find what maximizes your net revenue. When enabled on a product, the plugin splits incoming shoppers 50/50 between the baseline product and Variant B. Because variations are rendered server-side, shoppers experience zero layout shift (CLS: 0.00) or visual flicker. Real-time metrics—including Visitors, Add-to-Cart Rate, Conversion Rate, and Revenue Per Visitor (RPV)—are streamed to your dashboard, allowing you to crown the winning variation in 1-click.

= Does it work with caching plugins and CDNs? =
Yes. MarketPulse includes native cache-purging hooks for **LiteSpeed Cache, WP Rocket, W3 Total Cache, WP Super Cache, Breeze, and Cloudflare**. Whenever a price updates, the product page cache is flushed immediately.

= Do I need a credit card to activate the 14-day Free Trial? =
No. You do not need to provide any credit card or payment information to start your 14-day free trial. Simply click 1-Click Connect and your trial is active.

= Is customer order data or payment information shared with MarketPulse? =
Never. MarketPulse only tracks product catalog data (SKU, title, price, competitor links). We never access, read, or store customer names, addresses, payment information, or order history.
